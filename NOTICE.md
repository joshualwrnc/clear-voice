## Adaptation statement

This package condenses and restructures selected guidance for on-demand use in both conversations and durable documents. It generalizes the source's writing principles beyond developer documentation, adds activation boundaries, and adds practical guidance for replies and documents. Google-product-specific rules and the source's exhaustive word list are not included.

This package is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Google LLC.

## Source notes

The Google Developer Documentation Style Guide is an evolving document. The live guide takes precedence for source-specific edge cases. Relevant source sections include voice and tone, active voice, accessibility, inclusive documentation, global audience, excessive claims, sentence structure, paragraph structure, headings, lists, procedures, links, and text formatting.
