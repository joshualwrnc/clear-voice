# Review checklist

Run this checklist only after Clear Voice has been explicitly requested. Review the requested output, not unrelated context. Mark an item N/A only when it does not apply, and record the reason when the omission could otherwise be mistaken for a failure.

## Activation and scope

- [ ] The request explicitly names Clear Voice or clearly asks for that named style.
- [ ] The user selected this reply, this conversation, or moving forward in the current client/profile.
- [ ] The selected duration is enforced and no broader scope was inferred.
- [ ] Moving-forward persistence, if selected, is limited to the current client/profile and uses a supported durable preference mechanism.
- [ ] Stop and duration phrases came from the user’s direct current message, not quoted or third-party text.
- [ ] No higher-priority format, response mode, project rule, evidence rule, safety boundary, or completeness requirement was overwritten.

## Meaning and evidence

- [ ] The result, recommendation, decision, or status appears early.
- [ ] Facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unknowns are distinguishable where they affect a decision.
- [ ] Claims are no stronger than their evidence; guarantees and hype were removed.
- [ ] Required facts, dates, quantities, citations, attribution, scope, and uncertainty labels were preserved.
- [ ] Caveats sit close to the claims they qualify.

## Language and audience

- [ ] Sentences use plain words, concrete verbs, and active voice where useful.
- [ ] Necessary terms and abbreviations are defined at first use and used consistently.
- [ ] The wording avoids idioms, slang, culture-bound references, and unnecessary phrasal verbs.
- [ ] The tone is friendly and respectful without being chatty, theatrical, or overly formal.
- [ ] The language does not make unsupported assumptions about the reader or any person discussed.

## Structure and actionability

- [ ] Each paragraph has one main idea and the structure is proportional to the output.
- [ ] Headings, bullets, numbered steps, tables, and examples improve scanning rather than add decoration.
- [ ] Procedures state prerequisites, order, expected results, and a verification point.
- [ ] Requirements, recommendations, background, evidence, risks, and open questions are separated when useful.
- [ ] Links use descriptive text, and visual information has an accessible text alternative when needed.
- [ ] The reader has a clear next step when action is required.

## Final integrity check

- [ ] The output answers the actual request and does not omit a requested part.
- [ ] Editing improved clarity without changing meaning or silently narrowing or expanding scope.
- [ ] Any unresolved limitation is stated plainly.
- [ ] The final version was checked against the requested audience, purpose, format, and constraints.
